## What Was Found

The research identified a well-developed ecosystem of **separate, single-purpose tools** addressing different aspects of cryptocurrency safety:

- Liquidity locking platforms (Unicrypt, Team.Finance) — voluntary, L1 only
- Smart contract audit firms (CertiK, Hacken) — code security only, not scam prevention
- Blockchain analytics firms (Chainalysis, Elliptic) — post-transaction forensics only
- Identity and wallet naming (ENS) — naming only, no identity verification
- Operational security standards (CCSS) — exchange/business security, not token safety
- Regulatory frameworks (MiCA, IOSCO) — disclosure and oversight, not technical standards

---

## What Was Not Found

The research found **no prior published framework** that:

1. Combines protections against all four dominant crypto scam types (rug pull, pump-and-dump, fake platform, fake celebrity) into a single technical standard
2. Is designed specifically as a proposed exchange listing standard any exchange can choose to adopt
3. Uses a binary trust badge with no partial compliance pathway
4. Includes a cryptographic endorsement registry requiring zk-proof KYC for promoters and wallet-signed promotional content
5. Requires quarterly automated on-chain re-verification of compliance
6. Is published as an open framework under a Creative Commons license
7. Is authored by an independent third-party standards organization (not a commercial service provider)

Layer 4 — the Cryptographic Endorsement Registry — has **no prior art of any kind**. It is entirely original to NMA Consulting Group.

## Conclusion

Based on extensive research across public sources as documented in this repository, NMA Consulting Group concludes:

**The Crypto Trust Standard, as published in September 2026, is an original framework with no substantially identical prior published work.**

The closest existing frameworks — CCSS (operational security for crypto businesses) and MiCA (regulatory disclosure) — address categorically different problems for different audiences. No framework found combines all four CTS layers into a single named, bundled standard with the specific design characteristics of CTS.

NMA's publication of the CTS framework represents an original contribution to the cryptocurrency safety ecosystem, made in good faith, with full acknowledgment of the tools and frameworks that preceded it.
